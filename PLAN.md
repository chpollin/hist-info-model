# Plan

The dissertation and both requirement registers are connected by the [requirements integration](https://github.com/DigitalHumanitiesCraft/ivory-kleio/blob/codex/temporal-prototype/knowledge/requirements-integration.md). The conceptual foundation, proposed reconstructions, existing source examples and bounded executable evidence now have distinct roles. Consolidation does not validate the comparative ratings or adopt a general historical model.

## Existing research

[Dissertation context](knowledge/01-theory/Dissertation-Context.md) carries the established basis. [Requirements](knowledge/01-theory/Requirements.md) maintains R identifiers. [Project evidence](knowledge/03-examples/Project-Evidence.md) routes to existing dissertation examples and candidate project extensions. The [evaluation framework](knowledge/02-approaches/Evaluation-Framework.md) governs comparisons. The [paper outline](knowledge/05-paper/Paper-Outline.md) is an existing writing draft dependent on actual evidence.

The primitive reconstruction remains an optional explanatory hypothesis. A completed axiom system is not a prerequisite for evaluating the existing source workflows. The R and H registers are complementary views of the same undertaking, not two established independent projects.

## Open work

- Examine one existing dissertation case together with the expert. Fix its source location, interpretation, relevant R/H links, intended operation and independently stated expected behavior before a historical fixture is adopted.
- Resolve the specific semantic distinctions in the integrated register, especially coexistence versus exclusion, source versus interpretation time, explicit missingness and revision of dependent results.
- Assess the primitive reconstruction by its additional explanatory or practical contribution to that case. Retain unresolved dependencies as hypotheses.
- Extend the existing comparison only where a source passage, equivalent encoding and operation support a capability finding. Replace exploratory ratings with reviewed evidence before using them in a publication.
- Reconcile synthetic scenario inconsistencies and requirement assignments before presenting them as teaching evidence. Their invented content remains distinct from historical fixtures.
- Align the teaching website with the reviewed knowledge before publication. Its simplified projections and historical-data claims require a content review.
- Develop the existing paper argument from accepted findings. The current outline supplies no novelty or field-wide impossibility result.

The kernel's executable contracts and tests remain in [Ivory KLEIO](https://github.com/DigitalHumanitiesCraft/ivory-kleio/blob/codex/temporal-prototype/knowledge/plan.md). Repository ownership does not pre-empt scholarly acceptance or a later decision about subprojects.
